# Grocery checklist 🛒

A light routine. Do what's useful, skip what isn't. Just tell your Claude what you want.

## Weekly (5 minutes)

1. **Catch up on item notes.**
   Ask: *"Which grocery purchases still need item notes?"* (`grocery_candidates`)
   For each, tell Claude what we bought: *"Add 'chicken, rice, yogurt, bananas' to purchase 12345."*
   → Turns a dollar amount into a real record of what came home.

2. **Budget check.**
   Ask: *"How are we doing on groceries this month?"* (`grocery_budget_status`)
   If we're near the limit early in the month, that's the signal to ease off.

## Monthly (10 minutes)

3. **Where did the money go?**
   Ask: *"Grocery spend by store, this month"* and *"…vs last month."* (`grocery_spend_by_store`)
   Look for surprises — a store creeping up, a one-off big trip.

4. **Store strategy.**
   Ask: *"What do we buy at each store?"* (once item notes build up)
   Notice what's cheaper where. Adjust where we shop for what.

5. **One improvement.**
   Pick one thing to try next month — a bulk buy, a store swap, a category to watch.
   Tell Asif if you want a new tool to track it.

## Anytime

- *"How much do we spend on groceries a month, on average?"*
- *"Show me our biggest grocery trips this year."*
- *"Are we spending more at Costco or Sprouts?"*

---
*You can't break anything here — item notes only add, and your tools only touch groceries.*
